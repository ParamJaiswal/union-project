About This Tool

🎉 Festival Greeting Generator is a free, open-source web app that lets you create beautiful, personalized greeting cards for any occasion — in English and Hindi.

Features:

  • 6 festival templates (Diwali, Holi, Eid, Christmas, New Year, Winter)
  • Bilingual text — English + हिन्दी (Devanagari)
  • Upload your logo or decorative image
  • Full color customization
  • Download as high-resolution PNG — no watermarks
  • 100% client-side — your images never leave your browser

Built for: Side-income makers, small businesses, and anyone who wants to send meaningful digital greetings without expensive design tools.
